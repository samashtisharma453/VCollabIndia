About VCollab Platform

VCollab is India's leading hyper-local asset sharing marketplace. We bridge the gap between offline furniture & appliance retailers and urban renters across major metros including Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai, and Hyderabad.

Our ecosystem empowers local retailers to digitize inventory and earn recurring rental revenue with a transparent 15% platform commission model, while offering customers verified items, zero hidden deposits, and end-to-end relocation support.
